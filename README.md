# AI Systems Research

A collection of original articles, ideas and working theories on artificial intelligence, technology, software, systems, security and the future they are shaping.

This repository is not limited to one technical field. Many of the ideas explored here sit between disciplines and are easier to understand when software, cognition, economics, philosophy, infrastructure and social systems are treated as parts of the same structure.

The articles are written as attempts to map those structures, test assumptions and develop ideas beyond their first form.

## Articles

### [Beyond Software Distribution](./articles/beyond-software-distribution.md)

A proposal for distributing behavioral specifications instead of fixed applications, allowing trusted local models to construct, optimize and evolve software for each environment.

### Beyond Monolithic Agents: Designing a Self Learning AI Society

An exploration of AI systems that test and evolve their own organizational structures instead of operating through one permanently designed orchestration model.
