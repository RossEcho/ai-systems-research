### [Beyond Software Distribution](./articles/beyond-software-distribution/)

**When the Specification Becomes the Product**

Future software may be distributed as a behavioral specification rather than a fixed implementation. Trusted local system models could construct applications for their own environments, test alternative implementations and return valuable discoveries to the product owner.

This creates a model of software evolution built from local experimentation, centralized validation and explicit ownership.
